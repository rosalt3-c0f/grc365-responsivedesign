Since October/Halloween is coming up soon, here are some fun facts about famous candies!

Candies and Fun Facts
Name Type Facts
Candy Corn Mellowcreme The recipe has stayed the same over the past 100 years.
Reese's Chocolate The price used to only be 1 cent in the early 1930s.
Twix Chocolate The left and right twix bars were confirmed completely identical.
Snickers Chocolate In 2020 they made a gigantic snickers bar that weighed 2 tons.
100 Grand Chocolate A woman thought she'd get $100,000 but got this instead.
Skittles Fruit/Chew Any skittle that does not have an S on it gets destroyed.
Nerds Pellet Cherry/Orange and Strawberry/Grape were the original flavors.
Airheads Taffy The Airhead red balloon graphic has never been replaced before.
Tootsie Roll Chocolate/Chew This candy used to be sent to troops during WWI and WWII.
Kit-Kat Chocolate During WWII, the wrapper was blue and they used dark chocolate.
Sour Patch Kids Gummy Initially called Mars Men but switched names for marketing.
Haribo Gummy Children can trade acorns/chestnuts for gummy bears every Fall.
